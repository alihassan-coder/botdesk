# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want BotDesk to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or the language they're most comfortable in. Many contributors are learning as they go, and that's exactly how it should be.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and make it useful.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say thanks when someone helps.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions focused.

## What's not OK

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any other behaviour a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and events where you represent BotDesk.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email **alihassan.coder@gmail.com**. Every report is read, handled privately, and answered. You won't be penalised for reporting in good faith.

## What happens

Maintainers decide on a fair response. It can range from a private conversation or a warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, depending on what happened.
